Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in the mobile game Tower Ascend: Co-op Adventure. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Information We Collect

Tower Ascend: Co-op Adventure does not collect, store, or share any personal information directly from users.

The game does not require user registration and does not request personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.

Advertising

This game uses third-party advertising services provided by Google through Google AdMob.

AdMob may automatically collect certain information in order to display and improve advertisements. This information may include:

  • Device type and device information
  • Operating system version
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Basic app usage information

This information is used for purposes such as:

  • Displaying relevant advertisements
  • Improving ad performance
  • Preventing fraud or abuse
  • Analytics and ad delivery

The developer of Tower Ascend: Co-op Adventure does not have direct access to or control over the data collected by AdMob.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

Google AdMob operates under Google's own privacy policies. For more information on how Google handles user data, please review Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children’s Privacy

Tower Ascend: Co-op Adventure does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove such information.

Security

We value your trust in using our game. However, please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
