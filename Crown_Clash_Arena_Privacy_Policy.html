Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the mobile game Crown Clash Arena.

Information Collected by the Developer

The developer of Crown Clash Arena does not collect, store, transmit, process, or share any personal information or user data.

The game does not require user registration and does not ask for information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Local Save Data

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Advertising

Crown Clash Arena displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information to provide and improve advertising services. This may include:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Diagnostic information
  • Basic app interaction data related to advertising

The developer of Crown Clash Arena does not have access to or control over information collected by Google AdMob.

Third-Party Privacy Policy

For information about how Google collects and processes data, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Crown Clash Arena does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Any information collected through advertising services is governed by the policies of the respective third-party provider.

Security

No personal information is collected or stored by the developer. However, third-party advertising services may process data according to their own security and privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
